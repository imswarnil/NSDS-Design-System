// Assistant

Settings

How the assistant teaches you, what it is allowed to read, and what it keeps. Changes apply to your next question.

How it teaches

Default mode

Tutor explains and shows an example. Coach answers with a question first. Reviewer marks the code you paste against the lesson's standard.

Explain at this level

Sets the assumed starting point. It still adapts to what you have finished — this is where it starts, not where it stops.

Ask me a check question

After an explanation, end with one question to answer. Off means it just explains.

Always show sources

Every answer cites the lesson or doc it came from. Leaving this on is strongly recommended: an uncited answer is one you cannot check.

What it may read

The lesson I am on

Lets "explain this bit" mean the paragraph in front of you. Off, and every question needs its own context.

My course progress

Which lessons you have finished and your quiz scores. This is what makes a learning path fit you rather than a generic syllabus.

Files I attach

Apex classes, flow screenshots, error logs. Attachments are read for that question only and are never used for training.

Memory

Remember what I am working towards

Currently remembering: Platform Developer I by June, prefers examples before theory, works in an org with no dev sandbox.

Keep my chat history

History is stored so you can return to a conversation. Turn this off and each chat is discarded when you close it.

Your data

Export everything

A JSON file of every conversation, path and saved answer. Sent to your account email.

Forget what it remembers

Clears the three facts above. Your chat history and saved paths are kept.

Delete all conversations

Permanent, and it cannot be undone. Saved learning paths stay on your dashboard.

The assistant can be wrong. Check anything it says against the lesson it cites.
