Security and access

Profiles, permission sets, roles and sharing — in that order, because each one only makes sense given the last.

Who can see what

Salesforce answers access in two halves: what a user can do to an object, and which records of it they can see. Profiles and permission sets answer the first; the role hierarchy and sharing rules answer the second. Almost every access bug is someone solving one half with the other half’s tool.
