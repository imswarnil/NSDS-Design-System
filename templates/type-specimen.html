// N&M type system · specimen

Display speaks. Text explains. Mono keeps the records.

Three cuts, one voice. N&M Display (a Manrope cut) carries every heading; N&M Text (Nunito Sans) carries every sentence, set at 450, not 400, because this face's Regular reads grey rather than black; N&M Mono (Red Hat Mono) carries every index, timestamp and label. All three are latin-subset variable woff2s — about 77 KB for the whole family.

// The reading layer, at the real measure

Salesforce is a platform before it is a product, and the difference is the whole reason this curriculum exists. Learn the platform — the data model, the security model, the automation model — and every product built on top of it becomes a variation you already understand rather than a new thing to memorise.

Set at --size-body over --leading-body, capped at --measure-prose. Past roughly eighty characters a line the eye starts losing the return, which is a measurable reading cost and not a matter of taste.1Legibility research is unusually consistent on this: 45–75 characters is the comfortable band for continuous reading.Bringhurst, The Elements of Typographic Style
