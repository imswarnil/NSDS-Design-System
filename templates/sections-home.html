Recalculation is asynchronous · Security and access · 11 min

What actually happens on reparent

Sharing in Salesforce is materialized, not computed on read: the share tables hold a row for every grant, and a query checks rows, not rules. That design is why record access is fast at scale — and it has a consequence. When you change the thing the rows were derived from, the platform has to rebuild them, and it does that in a queue, not in your transaction.

So the sequence on a reparent is: your DML commits; a recalculation job is enqueued behind every other recalculation in the org; until it runs, the old share rows answer every access check.

Before you sign up

Who this is for, and who it is not

Worth your time if

  • You already touch a real org and keep hitting things you cannot explain.
  • You want the platform’s reasons, not a click-path you will forget by Thursday.
  • You are preparing for a certification and want it to be a side effect rather than the goal.
  • You read faster than you watch, and want the written version of everything.

Look elsewhere if

  • You have never opened Salesforce. Start with Trailhead’s basics — it is free and it is good at that.
  • You want a certification dump. There are no exam questions here, on purpose.
  • You need instructor-led sessions on a schedule. Everything here is self-paced.
  • You are looking for Marketing Cloud or CPQ. Not covered, and not planned this year.
