Questions

Before you start

Do I need a Salesforce license?

No — every lab runs in a free Developer Edition org. Lesson one walks you through creating it.

What is free and what is Pro?

120 of 150 training modules and every course's first section are free, in full, with no account. Pro adds the architecture and AI tracks, certification practice sets, and downloadable orgs for every lab.

Where should I start?

The roadmap. It begins at “what is an org” and tells you exactly which course is next at every step.
