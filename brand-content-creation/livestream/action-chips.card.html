Action chips — the five asks

One shape, five payloads, and a hard rule that carries the whole set: one filled chip per frame, and never more than two chips at once. A viewer who has to choose between five destinations goes to none of them. The filled chip is the ask of that moment; outline chips are reminders that something exists.

Do

  • One ask on screen at a time. The filled chip is the ask; everything else is a reminder.
  • Spell the destination out. “Link in the description” asks a phone viewer to leave the stream to find it.
  • Give every chip an exit rule and honour it. A chip with no timer becomes furniture, and furniture is what makes a frame look busy.

Never

  • A row of five social icons. One destination at a time, rotating; a viewer who has to choose goes nowhere.
  • Two filled chips. Two asks with equal weight is no ask at all.
  • Animated, looping or pulsing chips. Motion that repeats cannot be dismissed and cannot be ignored — it is the fastest way to make a stream unwatchable.
