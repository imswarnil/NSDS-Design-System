What a recorded lesson does NOT have. These belong to a live broadcast and nothing else. On a lesson they are noise the viewer cannot dismiss, and they date the video the moment the stream ends:

  • No LIVE badge — there is nothing live about a file.
  • No ticker, no chat, no comment strip — a recorded frame has no second audience to acknowledge.
  • No countdown, no “starting soon” — the lesson starts when the viewer presses play.
  • No rotating handle bug — one CTA, once, on the end card.
